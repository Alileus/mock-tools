# Code of conduct

Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions. Critique the work, explain your reasoning, and make room for people who are learning.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and sharing someone's private information are not welcome here.

Ali (@alileus) maintains this project and may remove content, close threads, or block accounts that violate these expectations. When possible, explain the problem and give people a chance to correct it.

Report abusive content using GitHub's report menu. You can also reach Ali through the [Alileus Discord](https://discord.com/invite/alileus); ask to speak privately before sharing sensitive details.
